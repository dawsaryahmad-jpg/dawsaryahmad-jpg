## Featured Project

### TexFlow Suite
A WhatsApp-first digital showroom designed to help businesses showcase products and receive structured customer orders without the friction of a traditional e-commerce checkout.

**How it works:**
1. A business gets a branded digital showroom.
2. Customers browse its products and services.
3. Customers select what they need.
4. Order details are prepared and sent directly to the business through WhatsApp.

The goal is simple: make discovering products and placing orders easier for both businesses and their customers.
